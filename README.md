## Background Overview
This dataset is one obtained from a PowerBI crash course, distributed to people attending it as a follow along project done in conjunction with the lecture.  As the course itself was an introductory course the information taught was the barebones of PowerBI. This dashboard is a further polishing of the data delving deeper into the obtainable insights.

Insights and recommendations are provided on the following key areas:
- Sales Trends Analysis: Evaluation of historical sales patterns, both globally and by region, focusing on Revenue, and Order Volume.
- Product Level Performance: An analysis of the various product lines in the dataset, understanding their impact on sales and returns.
- Employee Performance: Comparison of sales contributions across salespeople and countries.
- Regional Comparisons: An evaluation of sales and orders by region

An interactive PowerBI dashboard can be downloaded [*here*](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1Ej5u4x5_nirEIAWzD7TiUGxDIWJfx_5w/view?usp=drive_link)

## Data Structure Overview
The dataset contains 1,094 records across six fields: Boxes Shipped, Country, Date, Product, Sales, and Sales Person. As this was an introductory Power BI dataset, the dataset is relatively small and contains a single table.

### Product Performance
- Overviewing the product sales, all products have been averaging around the 3% ~ 5% of the total sales. The popularity of products being almost equal in sales amount.
- The overall top sales product "*Smooth Silky Salty*" ranked *highest* in sales during *March* and *May* with sales of RM31,010 and RM71,939 respectively.
- During *January* and *March* "*85% Dark Bars*" ranked the *lowest* in sales. January's in particular reached an all time low, earning only *RM273* in total.
- Drinking Coco recorded sales of *RM469* in *August*.

### Employee Performance
- The *employee* with the *best performance* during this 8 month period is *Ches Bonnel* making *RM320,901* in *sales*
-  While Wilone o'Kielt's performance suffered, making RM139,523(43.5% of top sales).
- Amongst the countries, the top 3 Countries that employees found to be able to make the most sales from during the 8 month period were India(6 employees), USA(5 employees) and Uk(5 employees). Indicative from counts of the country generating the most sales for employees.
- While the 3 Countries that employees found difficulty making sales from during these 8 month period were India(5 employees), New Zealand(5 employees) and USA (5 employees). Indicative from counts of the country generating the least sales for employees.
- Employees found that Australia was the region that had the lowest difficulty in making sales from(within this 8 month period), having the least amount of employees, 2 , finding difficulty in that region.


### Regional Performance
- Sales from each country is evenly distributed, the top country Australia made around RM1.14M in sales while the bottom last, New Zealand, made RM0.95M in sales. Both of which having a difference of around 26% between the two.
- Throughout the 8 months, July had 3 countries peaking their sales mainly - Canada, India and USA.
- After every country's top sales month the following month would have a 15% ~ 54% decrease to their sales. With New Zealand at 50% and UK at 54.4% respectively in the decrease of sales after their top sales.

#### Australia
Sales from Australia has an oscillating pattern, having sales peaking during January(RM187K) before falling in February and raising back up in March and plunging once more in April. Despite the oscillating pattern, the sales from Australia had a slow ebb to the sales across the 8 month period not reaching back up to the previous peak's sales amount.
