## Background Overview
This dataset is one obtained from a PowerBI crash course, distributed to people attending it as a follow along project done in conjunction with the lecture.  As the course itself was an introductory course the information taught was the barebones of PowerBI. This dashboard is a further polishing of the data delving deeper into the obtainable insights.

Insights and recommendations are provided on the following key areas:
- Sales Trends Analysis: Evaluation of historical sales patterns, both globally and by region, focusing on Revenue, and Order Volume.
- Product Level Performance: An analysis of the various product lines in the dataset, understanding their impact on sales and returns.
- Employee Performance: Comparison of sales contributions across salespeople and countries.
- Regional Comparisons: An evaluation of sales and orders by region

An interactive PowerBI dashboard can be downloaded [*here*](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1Ej5u4x5_nirEIAWzD7TiUGxDIWJfx_5w/view?usp=drive_link)

## Data Structure Overview
The dataset contains 1,094 records across six fields: Boxes Shipped, Country, Date, Product, Sales, and Sales Person. As this was an introductory Power BI dataset, the dataset is relatively small and contains a single table.

- Sales peaked during January with a total sales of RM896,105, with UK(21% of total sales), and Australia (20.9% total sales) being the top 2 contributers.
- Monthly sales declined after the January peak before recovering in June. Total sales reached its lowest during April with only RM674,051 total sales made, with Canada making up just 13% of total sales.

### Product Performance
- Overviewing the product sales, all products have been averaging around the *3% ~ 5%* of the *total sales*. This suggests that sales are *relatively evenly distributed* across the product range.
- The overall top sales product "*Smooth Silky Salty*" ranked *highest* in sales during *March* and *May* with sales of *RM31,010* and *RM71,939* respectively.
- During *January* and *March* "*85% Dark Bars*" ranked the *lowest* in sales. *January's* in particular reached an *all time low*, recording only *RM273* in sales.
- *Drinking Coco* recorded sales of *RM469* in *August*.

### Employee Performance
- *Ches Bonnel* recorded the highest sales contribution during the 8 month period, with *RM320,901* in sales
- *Wilone o'Kielt's* sales contribution suffered, recording the *lowest sales contribution* of *RM139,523*(43.5% of top sales).
- The top 3 countries that employees were able to record their highest country-level sales during the 8 month period were India(6 employees) followed by USA and UK(5 employees each).
- While the 3 countries that employees recorded their lowest country-level sales during the 8 month period were India, New Zealand and USA (5 employees each).
- Australia had the fewest employees recording it as their lowest-sales country, with just 2 employees.


### Regional Performance
- The top country Australia recorded approximately RM1.14M in sales, compared with RM0.95M for New Zealand the bottom ranking country, a difference of approximately RM190K.
- Throughout the 8 months, July had 3 countries peaking their sales mainly - Canada, India and USA.
- After every country's top sales month the following month would have a 15% ~ 54% decrease to their sales. With New Zealand at 50%  and UK at 54.4% respectively in the decrease of sales after their top sales month.

#### Australia
Sales from Australia has an oscillating pattern, having sales peaking during January(RM187K) before falling in February and raising back up in March and plunging once more in April. Despite the oscillating pattern, the sales from Australia had a slow ebb to the sales across the 8 month period not reaching back up to the previous peak's sales amount.

#### Canada
Canada's sales is akin to the composition of the big dipper, their sales hovering at the RM100k mark during the first three months January(RM144K), February(RM134K) and March(RM129K) before dipping for the next 3 months at around RM85K and peaking back up during July at RM159K.

#### India
India's sales fluctuation is quite tame compared to the rest of the regions. Starting off with January's RM143K before falling in February at RM84K, gradually increasing sales amount till reaching RM175K in July and settling back down during August. ## Recommendations
- Investigate the large variation in 85% Dark Bars sales. Although the product recorded the lowest sales in January and March, it later reached RM97,209 in August. This suggests that factors such as seasonality, promotions, or regional demand may be influencing its performance.
